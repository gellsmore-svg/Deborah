# Psychological: Experiential Avoidance (descriptive)

Descriptive Cairn model of not contacting aversive internal experience
and of safety behaviour that keeps the situation untested. Not a diagnosis
and not a treatment protocol.

## CONTEXT

- **Individual**: Person facing aversive internal or situational cues.
- **Cue**: Interoceptive or situational trigger.
- **Avoidance**: Experiential (inner), behavioral (not entering), safety (entering with neutralizing ritual).

## REQUIREMENTS

```
R1. Avoidance can target inner experience, overt situations, or both. [MUST]
R2. Safety behaviour can look like engagement while preventing the test. [MUST]
R3. Short-term relief can strengthen the loop. [SHOULD]
R4. Do not treat this description as a clinical protocol or a disorder name. [MUST]
```

## OUTCOMES

Temporary relief, maintained threat appraisal, and a narrower situation set —
or a tested situation if avoidance is not selected.

## PROCESS — Formal

```
PROCESS AvoidAversiveExperience (INPUT: aversive cue; OUTPUT: relief or contacted experience)
  1. APPRAISAL [TYPE: primary] of the cue as threat. [COGNITIVE, APPRAISAL, EMOTIONAL]
     CONDITION_MAP: HiTOP internalizing / ICD-11 anxiety or fear-related / RDoC potential threat
  2. AVOIDANCE [MODE: experiential] of the inner state. [AVOIDANT, TRANSDIAGNOSTIC]
  3. DECISION [ON: whether to enter the situation]
     stay out → behavioral avoidance
     enter with ritual → safety
  4. AVOIDANCE [MODE: safety] neutralizing ritual if entered. [AVOIDANT]
  5. FEEDBACK [FROM: short-term relief; VIA: dropped arousal] to future appraisal. [REGULATION]
  6. EMERGENT [TYPE: psychological]

  ITERATE [UNTIL: cue ends or situation is tested]
```

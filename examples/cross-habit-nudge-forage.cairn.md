# Cross-area: Habit × nudge friction × forage scent

Friction on the routine only works if the alternative patch has strong scent.

## CONTEXT

- **Person**: Cue-driven routine (badge, notification).
- **Architect**: Adds friction; may or may not scent an alternative.

## REQUIREMENTS

```
R1. HABIT is individual, not org REINFORCEMENT. [MUST]
R2. Weak alternative scent plus friction is frustration, not habit change. [MUST]
```

## OUTCOMES

Routine delayed, rerouted, or forced through anyway.

## PROCESS — Formal

```
PROCESS HabitFrictionForage (INPUT: cueing badge; OUTPUT: routine fired, rerouted, or stalled)
  1. HABIT [PHASE: cue] of the red badge. [BEHAVIORAL]
  2. FORAGE [SCENT: strong] of the badge as the current patch. [INTERACTIVE]
     HCI_TOUCHPOINT: notification badge
  3. NUDGE [TOOL: friction] on HABIT PHASE routine. [NUDGED]
  4. FORAGE [SCENT: weak] of the alternative path. [INTERACTIVE]
  5. HABIT [PHASE: routine] fires anyway, or stalls. [BEHAVIORAL]
  6. HABIT [PHASE: reward] if the routine still completes. [MOTIVATIONAL]
  7. EMERGENT [TYPE: psychological]
```
